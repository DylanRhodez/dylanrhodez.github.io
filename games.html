SIMPLE SNAKE is a classic snake game by Dylan Rhodez. Eat the food to grow and score points, and avoid the walls and your own tail.

Controls: arrow keys or W, A, S, D to move, and Space to pause. Hold the key for your direction to boost. You can change the speed and boost in the game's Settings.

Version 1.0 (2026/10/17). Needs a keyboard, so it isn't playable on phones.
